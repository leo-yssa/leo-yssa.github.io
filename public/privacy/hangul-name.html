Privacy Policy

App: Hangul Name (com.dotorio.hangul_name) · Effective: November 1, 2026

1. Information we collect

Hangul Name has no accounts. The names and Korean text you type are processed on your device only and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Images you choose to share are created on your device and sent only to the app you pick.

2. Text-to-speech

The "Listen" button uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Depending on your device settings, that engine may be provided by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, IDFA), device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can also opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

4. Name data updates

The app downloads updated name lists from portfolio.dotorio.com. No personal data is sent; the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard access logs such as IP addresses.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

yssa@kakao.com

7. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
